F9P receiver configured over the UART, Write once after a new receiver or firmware

F9P UART1 to the ESP32 at 460800 baud: GGA, VTG, RMC, GSA and GST at 10 Hz, GSV, NAV-SAT and NAV-SIG at 1 Hz. The RS-232 output picks its own rates from these. USB gets the same NMEA. F9P UART2 is left as it is. The ESP32 finds the receiver at 460800, 115200, 38400 or 9600 baud and follows it to 460800 after a write.

Reset to defaults?

All settings will be returned to their startup defaults and the ESP32 will restart.

The WiFi hotspot settings are not changed, so you will still be able to reach this page over the ESP32's own hotspot. Reset those manually if you need to.

Waiting for restart...
